# 🧩 English Island Adventure - Interactive Quiz Bank

This document contains a collection of interactive-style quiz questions designed for elementary students. These can be used for offline practice, classroom activities, or future app features.

---

## 🟢 Level 1: Beginner (Word Wizard & Tracing)

### 1. Fill in the Missing Letters (Animals)
Complete the words by filling in the blanks.
- C _ T (Hint: Meow!)
- D _ G (Hint: Woof!)
- B _ _ R (Hint: Toby is one of these!)
- F _ S H (Hint: It swims in the sea.)

### 2. Multiple Choice: Colors
What color is the sky on a sunny day?
- A) Red
- B) Green
- C) Blue
- D) Yellow

## 🟡 Level 2: Intermediate (Matching & Scramble)

### 3. Word Scramble: Fruits
Unscramble these letters to find the fruit!
- **P P L E A** -> _________
- **N A N A B A** -> _________
- **N G E O R A** -> _________
- **G P E R A** -> _________

### 4. Match the Pairs (English to Indonesian)
Draw a line to connect the English word to its Indonesian meaning.
- **Apple**          •          • **Buku**
- **School**         •          • **Apel**
- **Book**           •          • **Sekolah**
- **Teacher**        •          • **Guru**
